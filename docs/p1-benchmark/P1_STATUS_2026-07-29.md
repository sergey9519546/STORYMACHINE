### 3. The structural signal diagnosis (commit 2ccabfc)

Root cause of CLIMAX_RELOCATE AUC 0.490: **every field in
`ScreenplaySceneRecord` is derived from that scene's own text, so
reordering scenes preserves every field.** No formula on those fields
can detect reordering. Verified across 5 candidate signals:
`tightestScene` (sceneIdx not position), `arcCoherence` (±0.1 wobble),
`escalationMonotonicity` (stuck at 0.500), `payoffSetupIds` (content-
detected, 0 inversions), `peakPosFrac` (preserved through reorder).

### 4. The dead-end on peakPosFrac (this status)

Tested the most promising candidate signal — peak-position-fraction —
on the train set. Under CLIMAX_RELOCATE it barely moves (only 1 of 8
train scripts shows meaningful change). Root cause confirmed:
`suspenseDelta` is recomputed from per-scene content and is preserved
through reorder. Any signal built on per-scene intensity inherits the
blindness.

## What this means

**The P1 structural-deduction bet cannot be won at the formula layer.**
It requires analyzer-layer work: a signal computed *after* scene
reordering, reading relative position between scenes. The candidates
named in the diagnosis (scene-to-scene intensity delta, forward-
reference density, local-context emotional shift) all require new
fields in the analysis pipeline.

This is a larger, separately-scoped effort than a formula constant
change. It is the right next P1 work, but it is not a 30-minute tweak.

## What was NOT shipped, and why

No formula change was committed. I could tune a constant to nudge
SHUFFLE from 0.771 to ~0.81 on the train set, but without a new signal
that would be overfitting — it would not generalize to the held-out
test set. The pre-registration protocol (§10) forbids this explicitly.

## P1 next steps (for the next session)

1. **Implement a position-reading signal at the analyzer layer.** The
   strongest candidate: compute scene-to-scene intensity *delta*
   (`intensity[i] − intensity[i−1]`) as a new field, *after* scene
   segmentation. This is positional by construction — a relocated
   climax produces a large positive delta at position 2 then negative
   deltas after, which is structurally distinct from a script that
   builds gradually.
2. **Measure the new signal's discrimination** on the train set (does
   it move under relocate?). If yes, wire into a bounded deduction.
3. **Tune on train, check on val, evaluate once on test.**
4. **Expand the corpus** beyond animation (live-action features may
   carry different position signals).
