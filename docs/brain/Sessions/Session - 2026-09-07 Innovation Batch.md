# Session — 2026-09-07: The Innovation Batch, and the Rebuild

**Heading:** "2026-09-07 — the innovation batch, and the rebuild that cost it
its record." The owner asked for the project to be pushed forward without
steering away from what STORYMACHINE is. Two read-only discoveries chose the
direction; five build lanes followed ([[Audit - 2026-09-07 Innovation Batch]]),
none passing review on the first pass.

**What landed on main (9b199b72 → c181dfe1):**

- [[Gate - Public Benchmark]] — P1's instrument: a degradation AUC over the
  32 distributable scripts on every CI run, with a positive control the
  review demanded. Its measured finding — the health formula paid a writer
  to delete a third of their scenes — became the scoring branch.
- `npm run verify-report` — P3 closed with an offline verifier; the review
  showed it printing VERIFIED on forged reports in two of three shapes before
  the route's own schema and a body-vs-verify-block cross-check closed it.
- The feature-length loop — the first feature-length fixture in the
  repository (231 scenes, CC0), the React render loop fixed at its cause,
  one jump control per located finding, root-cause cards that agree with
  their expanders; a browser step made deterministic and a shape guard given
  two tiers by the review.
- The producer's exports — one root-cause pipeline for every surface, an
  evidence-gated logline, a one-page producer tier with page numbers that
  match the PDF, a symmetric percentile comparability gate, collapsed scene
  lists. Built twice: the first build was lost unpushed in the sandbox
  rebuild and rebuilt under §7.

**What did not land, by design:** [[Branch - Feature-Length Defects]] and
[[Branch - Feature-Length Saturation Only]] — three review rounds to
MERGE-READY-FOR-OWNER, receipts PENDING, the owner's order corrected in
[[Owner - R5 Measurement and Merge]].

**The rebuild.** Between the exports lane's MERGE verdict and its merge the
sandbox was rebuilt: every worktree, the scratch directory, every local
audit tag, and the exports lane's two unpushed commits were erased, with
five lanes' review files and both discovery reports. The surviving final
messages are committed verbatim under `docs/audits/2026-09-07-innovation/`;
`docs/LANE_STANDARD.md` §7 is the rule that followed. See [[Patterns]].
