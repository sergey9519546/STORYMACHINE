# Measurement — WHAT_THE_SCORE_DOES_2026-07-28

**Question:** a plain-language, non-technical statement of what the current
score actually measures on real screenplays, and what it has never been
shown to measure — the active-work prompt's definition-of-done artifact,
for a writer or decision-owner who needs to know what they can and cannot
trust before running a draft.

**What it measures:** its numbers are reproducible by running
`REAL_SCRIPT_CORPUS_DIR="../real-script-corpus" npm run measure-real`
against the local produced-feature corpus (73 scripts at time of writing).
Full evidence and method are in
[[Measurement - REAL_SCRIPT_DISCRIMINATION_2026-07-28]]; this document is
its plain-language companion.

**What was NOT reproduced:** by design, this document reports what the
score has never been shown to do (human-agreement evidence) as plainly as
what it has.
